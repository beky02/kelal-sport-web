# 01 — Screens

Every player screen, with what it is for, the states it must have, what it reads, what it offers and
where to look at it. Screenshots come from `pnpm ui` (`test-results/ui/<name>-<lang>-<device>.png`, en
and am, 375 and 1440 px); the names below are the `screens.spec.ts` entries. The design project's pages
are named as they are there.

Every screen with data has four states — loading, empty, error, loaded — and, where a guest can reach
it, a guest state. Loading keeps the screen's shape with skeletons; nothing jumps when data lands.
Errors name what failed and offer a retry; a Problem the player can fix offers the fix (05-errors).
